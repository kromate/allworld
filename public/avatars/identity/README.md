# Creator identity model provenance

The pack in this directory is fetched only by `identityWorker.ts` during a creator fit. The fitter code is bundled with that lazy worker from `src/features/avatar/identity/gnm/`. Neither location contains photos or people-specific parameters.

- `gnm-identity24.pack.gz` is the lab's 24-identity-component reduction of the Google GNM v3 web head, originally distributed by `xrblocks/assets-gnm` at commit `134feb02b11fa642a43ff5e7e880246255a74e86` as `gnm_head_web.bin` (SHA-256 `fd19f46eef6f8bfb725fceab581e1bc8837209997ca3fd43f3c1735003c86961`). The reduced uncompressed GNMW container is 2,193,736 bytes, SHA-256 `3a8553ee5587bf4a6a6117cf2c7be50a51b925834c5b2dd0c39abdf5039e426a`. Its deterministic gzip is 1,286,019 bytes, SHA-256 `0009540d59e066602b40328fd2518619f07876bc0e49c9c35762413a6cef7959`. The worker verifies the uncompressed hash before fitting. The lab's `verify-reduction.mjs` compared six deterministic coefficient vectors against the full model within the retained subspace.
- `src/features/avatar/identity/gnm/GNMModel.js`, `FaceFit.js`, and `FaceCorrespondence.js` derive from the Apache-2.0 Google XR Blocks avatar lab at commit `c737bdfd0968777dcd3a2c8e213d71340b951fa1`. `FaceFit.js` was modified in the likeness lab for independently aligned shared-frame fitting, then reduced here to the identity fitter and changed to use the 159 rigid correspondences addressable by the product's 468-point mesh. `FaceCorrespondence.js` changes the required frame length from 478 to 468. `GNMModel.js` matches the upstream SHA-256 `e9619714dc5a975f66fc8c9cef76220441fd8e644cf9d14e43022b4705579c5f`.
- `FaceFit.js` also credits Eduardo Alvarado's [GNM Webcam Puppet](https://github.com/edualvarado/gnm-webcam-puppet/tree/888b83dca52e16bb84841337ef27a0f93aff3640) for the differential fitting method. Its Apache-2.0 notice is copied as `Apache-2.0-GNM-Webcam-Puppet.txt` (SHA-256 `58d1e17ffe5109a7ae296caafcadfdbe6a7d176f0bc4ab01e12a689b0499d8bd`).
- The Apache-2.0 notices for XR Blocks, GNM, `assets-gnm`, and GNM Webcam Puppet are adjacent. The model-code and model-weight grants are Apache-2.0; this does not establish rights to the raw training scans, which are not present here.

The fitted identity parameters are an experiment until real 468-mesh and controlled turn captures, visible head previews, and device memory are evaluated. The existing `FaceScan` shape and texture remain the baseline. A persisted identity will need a separate versioned field with model hash, calibration revision, bounded 24 coefficients, and a derived head representation or renderer support; the current `{texture, mesh, views}` fields cannot carry that contract safely.
