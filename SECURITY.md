# Security and privacy

No private security reporting contact has been selected for the public repository.
The owner must add one before publication. Until then, use an existing direct channel to the
owner if you have one. Do not put exploit instructions, private state, face data, or live
credentials in a public issue. No email address or security team is invented here.

## Sensitive boundaries

- Match me saves editable appearance without pixels. An account's separately chosen photo
  face sends a reduced crop and shape points to the service. Preserve audience filters,
  friends-only defaults, deletion, and the guest prohibition on saving photo faces.
- The world stores a chosen coarse area. A game-scene position is not the player's precise
  real-world position. Do not save or reveal device coordinates.
- Named local actors are development identities. Hosted guests and accounts require their
  own validated capabilities or verified identity exchange. A failed hosted exchange cannot
  fall back to the local actor query.
- Service modules decide coins, fares, rewards, ownership, match results, and permissions.
  Preserve blocks and viewer-specific filters on responses, events, occupancy, and visits.
- Saved worlds, guest credentials, environment files, issuer configuration, and private
  evidence do not belong in a public source package. Ignore patterns alone are not an export audit.
- A successful state-changing reply must follow the configured durable persistence barrier.
  Retries preserve intent IDs and return receipts rather than duplicate changes.

The public source package contains no live service credentials. A private hosted playtest
has existed separately. Consult [hosting boundaries](docs/HOSTING.md) for source-level limits,
not for a claim that the hosted deployment is accepted or publicly released.
Email, WhatsApp, and push adapters prepare messages without sending them. Voice remains
unverified between two real people. Map and model endpoints have their own usage terms.

Read [the architecture](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) and [source export gate](docs/SOURCE-EXPORT.md)
when reviewing a change that crosses these boundaries.
